A digital photo is a grid of tiny squares called pixels. This one has 300 rows and 480 columns, 144,000 pixels. Each pixel stores three numbers, for red, green and blue light, each from 0 (none) to 255 (full)<Cite c="Gonzalez & Woods, 2018" />. Here a pixel's brightness is the average of its three numbers. 0 is black, 255 is white.
